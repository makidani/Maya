ABOUT US

More Than a Massage. A Real Escape.

Maya Massage is not a big hotel chain — it's a small, family-run place in Atelas, Addis Ababa. We know our guests by name, remember how you like your oil, and always have a warm towel waiting.

Our therapists train for years before they ever touch a client. We only use natural oils — no cheap chemicals, no shortcuts. Every treatment is adjusted to your body, not copied from a manual.

Can't come to us? We come to you. Our outdoor service brings the same professional massage to your home, hotel, or office — anywhere in Addis Ababa.
